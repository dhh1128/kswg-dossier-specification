[[def: revocation-operator, revocation operator]]

~ An m-ary operator placed in an edge group's `o` field to define the number of endorsements required to revoke a dossier, configurable independently of the issuance threshold. The revocation threshold operators are `RMxN` and `RMxQ`, which mirror the mechanics of the issuance [[ref: threshold-operator, threshold operators]] `MxN` and `MxQ`.
