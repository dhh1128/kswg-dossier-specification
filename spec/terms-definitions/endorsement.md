[[def: endorsement, endorsements]]

~ A signed ACDC by which a candidate endorser asserts approval of a subject SAID (typically a dossier) for a named act (`issue` or `revoke`). A signed endorsement is an authenticated act that counts toward a joint-issuance [[ref: threshold-operator, threshold operator]]. An unsigned endorsement carries no authenticated decision and serves only as a placeholder reserving a slot for a named candidate. Contrast with [[ref: declination]].
