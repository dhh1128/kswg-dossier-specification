[[def: endorsement, endorsements]]

~ An ACDC, anchored by its issuer, by which a candidate records a disposition toward a subject SAID (typically a dossier) for a named act (`issue` or `revoke`). A single Endorsement schema serves both dispositions: with `disp` of `endorse` it is an authenticated approval whose slot weight (`w`) is added toward a joint-issuance [[ref: threshold-operator, threshold operator]]; with `disp` of `decline` it is a [[ref: declination]]. An unanchored endorsement carries no authenticated decision and serves only as a placeholder reserving a slot for a named candidate.
